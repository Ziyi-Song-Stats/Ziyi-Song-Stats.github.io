I am a CosmicAI Postdoctoral Fellow in the [Department of Statistics and Data Sciences](https://stat.utexas.edu/) at The University of Texas at Austin and the [NSF-Simons AI Institute for Cosmic Origins](https://cosmicai.org/), where I work with [Alessandro Rinaldo](https://arinaldo.github.io/) and [Arya Farahi](https://afarahi.github.io/) in the Explainable Universe working group. I also collaborate with [Peter Müller](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=iJ0qZckAAAAJ&hl=en).

I received my PhD in Statistics from the [University of California, Irvine](https://www.stat.uci.edu/), advised by [Michele Guindani](https://www.micheleguindani.info/) (now at UCLA) and [Weining Shen](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/weinings/). Since my visit to the Department of Economics, Management and Statistics at the University of Milano-Bicocca in Milan, Italy, in 2025, I have worked with [Federico Camerlenghi](https://sites.google.com/unimib.it/camerlenghi-federico/) and [Mario Beraha](https://sites.google.com/view/marioberaha). I also collaborate with [Falco J. Bargagli-Stoffi](https://www.falcobargaglistoffi.com/) (UCLA).

## Research Interests

I develop machine learning and statistical methods for reliable AI in science, with applications in astrophysics and cosmology, oncology, and neuroscience.

- **Generative AI for science:** deep generative models, flow matching, rectified flow, simulation-based inference, and multi-fidelity learning.
- **Probabilistic machine learning:** Bayesian nonparametrics, posterior theory, and exact MCMC algorithms.
- **Causal machine learning:** heterogeneous treatment effects in clinical trials and survival analysis.

During my PhD, I worked on Bayesian methods, Bayesian nonparametrics, random partition models, random measures, point processes, repulsive mixture models, spatio-temporal and functional data analysis, causal inference, and biomedical applications.
